## Connector endpoint

- **Endpoint:** Criteo Ads · `statistics`
- **Default destination table:** `criteo_ads_statistics`
- **Unique keys:** `CampaignId`, `AdvertiserId`, `AdsetId`, `AdId`, `Day`
- **Time series:** yes, loaded by date range
